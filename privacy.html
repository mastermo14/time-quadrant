Time Quadrant Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026
Developer: Quiet Orbit Studio
Privacy contact: maheshmohanty52@outlook.com

Information used on your device
Time Quadrant stores the tasks and settings you enter on your device. This includes task titles, notes, categories, tags, due dates, recurrence, priorities, subtasks, completion history and appearance preferences. This information is used to organize your tasks, calculate progress and schedule reminders.

Collection and sharing
The current app does not require an account and does not send your task content to a developer-operated server. It does not include advertising or analytics services in its application code, sell task data, or send assignments to other users. Local notifications are scheduled on your device; they do not use a remote push service.

Permissions and reminders
With your permission, the app displays notifications and may use vibration or haptic feedback. Reminder text may appear on your lock screen depending on your Android notification settings. You can disable notifications through your device settings. The app does not require access to your contacts, photos, microphone or location for its task-management features.

Storage and security
Task data is stored using local app storage. The app does not provide its own database encryption, app password or cloud synchronization. Device security and any operating-system backup or restore behavior depend on your device settings and platform provider. Protect your device with a screen lock and avoid entering sensitive information you do not want stored locally.

Retention and deletion
Task data remains in local storage until you delete it or clear the app's data. You can delete individual tasks within the app. Android Settings lets you clear all local app storage. Uninstalling usually removes local app data, but operating-system backups may retain or restore copies under your backup settings. Manage those copies through your platform's backup controls. There is no developer-hosted task database for us to delete or restore.

Support communications
If you contact the developer by email, your email address and the information you choose to send are handled to respond to your request. Email is handled through the relevant email providers. Do not send passwords or sensitive task content. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to address the request and any applicable obligations; contact the privacy address to request deletion of correspondence.

Privacy policy website
This public policy page is hosted by GitHub Pages. Visiting it sends normal web request information, such as your IP address, to GitHub as the hosting provider. GitHub handles this information under its own privacy statement at https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement. The page itself uses no analytics scripts, advertising, cookies or external fonts. Opening this page does not upload your task list.

Children
Time Quadrant is a general task organizer. The app does not ask for a child's age or operate an account registration service.

Changes and contact
We may update this policy when the app's features or data practices change. The effective date identifies the current policy. For questions about privacy or support correspondence, contact maheshmohanty52@outlook.com.
